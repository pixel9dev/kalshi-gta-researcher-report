Kalshi has already demonstrated massive retail appetite across *GTA VI* release-date and PS5 retail-price contracts. 3. **Microstructure Behavioral Bias Adjustments:** Retail traders on pop-culture markets systematically **overbuy round-number hype tails** (e.g., sub-15h blind speedruns or 5M single-platform Twitch concurrency) while **underpricing structural accounting mechanics** (e.g., 100% of digital pre-orders recognizing in Take-Two's Day-1 24-hour revenue headline, and Thursday->Friday two-day PTO bridges).
