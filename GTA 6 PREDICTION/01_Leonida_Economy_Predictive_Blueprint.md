## 2. Five Structural Predictions for Leonida's Financial Mechanics

### Prediction 1: Two-Tier Currency Friction ("Dirty Stash Cash" vs. "Clean Banked USD")
- **Anchor Evidence:** Lucia begins under Leonida Penitentiary parole/ankle-monitor scrutiny, while **Boobie Ike** is explicitly profiled by Rockstar as converting street earnings into legitimate Vice City real estate, a recording studio, and the *Jack of Hearts* strip club.
- **Predicted Mechanic:** Raw robbery/smuggling bills sit in physical safehouse/trunk stashes (*RDR2* camp-ledger evolution) and cannot enter clean financial brokerages above strict FINCEN-parody thresholds without passing through **cash-laundering businesses** (clubs, car washes, studios) charging a **10%–20% laundering vigorish** (reducible via business upgrades).
- **Min-Max Flip from GTA V:** While mid-story businesses in *GTA V* were `-99.4%` NPV traps, **unlocking the highest-efficiency Vice City laundering front early in Act II will be mandatory to feed clean capital into Leonida's markets.**

### Prediction 2: Dual Financial Markets — Corporate Equities + Parody Meme/Crypto Tokens
- **Anchor Evidence:** Heavy Vice City fintech/crypto satire and viral vertical-video social feeds across Trailers 1 & 2.
- **Predicted Mechanic:** Two distinct asset classes on Lucia & Jason's smartphones:
  1. **Corporate Equities (`LomBank / LCN`):** Bounded `±35%` to `±100%` reactions to Raul Bautista bank heists and Ambrosia/Vice City corporate sabotage.
  2. **Viral Meme/Crypto Coins:** Driven by in-world influencer stunts and stranger side-missions, featuring violent **`+250%` pump -> `-92%` rug-pull** trajectories within 12–18 in-game hours.
- **Guide Fallacy Forecast:** Clickbait Week-1 guides will tell players to "HODL" scripted rug-pull coins. Our Stream Deck tick-logger will map exact peak hours via manual-save binary search.

### Prediction 3: `INT64` Wallet Upgrade Paired with Anti-Sleep-Scum Circuit Breakers

### Prediction 4: Raul Bautista's "Risk-for-Cut" Dynamic Heist Frontier
